## Patterns and insights
- **H1b supported (CLIP measured, probes-20261021/clip-metric/report.md):** on 1,005 cohort rooms (identical cameras/settings), GT beats the 27B raw layout in only 43.7-53.3% of rooms across CLIP ViT-L/14 and SigLIP-so400m, text and top/perspective views; in the perspective view GT is even significantly lower (-0.20 [-0.36,-0.06]); Spearman(GT-raw, raw violations) |rho| <= 0.05; AUC checker-pass vs fail 0.46-0.58; image similarity to the GT render rho -0.09..0.00. Shuffled furniture text moves the score by only 0.25-0.35 vs a 2.5-4.1 between-room spread: the score is room type + asset look + framing, not arrangement. On R20, sign agreement with the by-eye S2-vs-S1 labels 3-9 of 13 (chance ~6.5).
- Literature (literature/survey.md, 15 papers): text-image scores do not track layout quality. Kendall tau with human ratings: CLIPScore 0.06, BLIPScore 0.07, VQAScore 0.13, GPT-4o top-down 0.27, GPT-4o multi-view first-person 0.39 (VIPScene arXiv 2506.20601 Table 2, verified). VQAScore 58.6% agreement with human pairwise preference (Procedural Scene Programs); CLIPScore Cohen kappa 0.02 per scene (LEGO-Eval). CLIP is near chance on spatial relations (ARO VG-Relation 0.59 vs 0.50 chance; What'sUp 26-31% vs 25%).
- Others use CLIP for prompt alignment (text-to-scene), not for arrangement quality; geometry is scored symbolically and quality by a VLM judge or humans. Symbolic checks agree with humans 94%/83% vs 59%/47% for a VLM judge (SceneCritic).
- Literature thresholds for companion relations: next_to 0-0.5 m, near 0.5-1.5 m (SceneEval/Holodeck); facing within 30 deg full credit, 75 deg pass (Roomer); passage/dining clearance 0.60 m.

- **H3 partly supported (probes-20261021/s2f/REPORT.md):** S2-F keeps 98.9%/101.6% (dev/cohort) of S2's checker gain and 97.8%/99.3% of its strict gain, companion pairs kept 0.853 -> 0.966 (cohort; nightstand 0.81 -> 0.94, dining chair 0.82 -> 0.98, sofa group 0.88 -> 0.99), median move 1.00 -> 0.72 m; but retention stays below S1 (end to end 0.943 vs 0.974; GT-based 0.735 vs 0.757) -> the 'retention >= S1' part fails. By eye (second rater, non-blind, 40 rooms): S2-F vs S1 better 14 / same 19 / worse 7; vs S2 better 7 / same 32 / worse 1 (25 of 40 layouts identical to S2).
- Rater noise is large: the two by-eye raters disagree on 5 of 12 shared S2-vs-S1 rooms. With a position-biased judge as well, no single visual signal here is reliable yet; human pairwise labels are needed to calibrate.
- Remaining visual failures need rules S2-F lacks: toilet/sink corner, dressing table + chair, generic chair/table, and a TV-stand rule stricter than a facing cone (distance band 1.5-4 m and not adjacent to the bed side).

- Judge interim (101 ok / 18 busy calls): ref>raw 13/20 (65%, H1a bar 70% not met); S1 vs S2 10/10 with 75% first-position picks; flipped 8/10 consistent; S2F vs S1 (set A) 12-8; S2F vs S2 (set A) 6 wins / 14 ties / 0 losses. Agreement with by-eye 9/13 (S1 vs S2).
- **Decision (interim):** adopt checker+strict (geometry) + symbolic companion score as the primary metrics; GPT pairwise only as a secondary, order-flipped, human-calibrated signal; CLIP rejected. Adopt S2-F over S2.

- **Training-target finding (exploratory, smoke 200 test rooms, probes-20261021/teacher-companions/smoke):** the delivered pose-only teacher that builds the placer's SFT targets (pose_targets.repair 200/4, prior sizes) keeps only 0.764 of GT companion pairs vs 0.909 when only sizes are swapped (sofa group 0.33, TV facing 0.60, nightstand 0.88). The model is trained on geometry-repaired targets that break furniture function -> proposed training direction 1 (function-preserving targets), then 2 (function-aware GDPO with symbolic rewards), 3 (anchor-relative coordinates). FULL RUN (8,647 test rooms, teacher-companions/test8647): pairs kept with prior sizes only 0.915 -> after teacher 0.795 (teacher-ok rooms); 21-50 objects 0.848 -> 0.596; by role sofa group 0.49, TV facing 0.82, nightstand 0.88, desk chair 0.84, dining chair 0.93; teacher success 0.915 overall but 0.807 at 21-50 and 0.608 at 51+ (dense rooms drop out of the training pool).

- **Judge v1 final (judge/report.md, 152 ok calls):** ref>raw 13/7 (0.65 [0.43,0.82]); ref>S1 set B 11/1/8 (0.58); S1 vs S2 10/10, first position 75%; flipped 8/10 same winner; S2F vs S1: A 12/0/8, B 7/3/10 (pooled 19/3/18); S2F vs S2: A 6/14/0, B 4/16/0 (never loses, 30 ties, 10 decisive). The single-vote judge separates layouts poorly -> H1a refuted; H2 refuted (S2 = S1 at 50%).
- **Synthesis (outer loop):** (1) neither CLIP nor a single-vote VLM judge is a valid layout-quality endpoint here; symbolic geometry + symbolic companion relations are the only signals that behave sensibly, and they disagree with nothing the judge resolves. (2) S2-F weakly dominates S2 (same checker/strict, more companions, judge never worse) -> replace S2 with S2-F. (3) Versus S1, S2-F buys +0.06 checker and +0.28 strict (the backend's admission) at visual parity by the judge -> adopt S2-F after S1, keep companion rules growing. (4) The same geometry-only repair logic sits inside the training-target teacher and removes 12 points of companion pairs (0.915 -> 0.795), most at 21-50 objects and for sofa groups -> the training-side lever is function-preserving targets, then function-aware RL with symbolic rewards (not CLIP/VLM rewards).

## Phase 2 (started 2026-10-21): task-aware placement (user approved adding task text + required_relations)
- Upstream reality (EmbodiedGen outputs, 23,187 SemanticSpecs): `task` is a short template — room-type names ("dining_room" 1,755, "bathroom" 1,368, ...) or "verb obj1, obj2" ("find shelf", "watch_tv media_console, tv"); 95.3% have no required_relations, 4.6% have one, all `on` supports (tv on tv_stand, sink on kitchen_counter). EntityReq.reason is a tag (task_required / task_dependency), not text. So rich LLM-written task text would not match upstream; the usable signal is WHICH objects are task objects (they must be reachable: downstream A3 required set) plus rare `on` pairs. FastFill's model input currently drops all of it (contract.py).
- Headroom (cohort 1,009; 2 hash-picked floor categories per room as task objects; probes-20261021/task-reach/cohort): task-scoped admission GT 0.525, raw 0.406, S1 0.538, S2F 0.845 (vs all-category 0.812). Of the 156 S2F rooms failing task-scoped admission, 77 fail only on task-object reachability (A3), 109 involve it -> ceiling +0.08 to +0.11.
- LabBuilder-style blocker push scoped to task objects (task-reach/cohort-nav): fixes 16/126 (+0.016), no checker regressions, companions 255/264 kept; 87 still A3 -> blocker pushing is too weak; next = relocate the unreachable task object itself inside S2-F with reachability in the objective.
