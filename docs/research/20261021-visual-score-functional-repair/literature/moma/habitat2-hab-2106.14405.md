# Habitat 2.0: Training Home Assistants to Rearrange their Habitat
- Authors: Andrew Szot, Alex Clegg, Eric Undersander, Erik Wijmans, Yili Zhao, John Turner, Noah Maestre, Mustafa Mukadam, Devendra Chaplot, Oleksandr Maksymets, Aaron Gokaslan, Vladimir Vondrus, Sameer Dharur, Franziska Meier, Wojciech Galuba, Angel Chang, Zsolt Kira, Vladlen Koltun, Jitendra Malik, Manolis Savva, Dhruv Batra. 2021 (arXiv 28 Jun 2021; NeurIPS 2021 venue unverified from page). https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.14405 (read via ar5iv)
- What: H2.0 simulator (25,734 / 7,699 SPS idle/interact on 8 GPUs) + ReplicaCAD (6 rooms of FRL apartment, 92 objects; 105 layouts = 5 macro x 20/21 micro variations, counts inconsistent in paper; fridge, counter cabinets, drawers articulated) + Home Assistant Benchmark (HAB).
- Tasks: TidyHouse (5 objects, 6 receptacles, no articulation, 5000 steps); PrepareGroceries (fridge fully open, 2 out + 1 in, 4000 steps); SetTable (bowl from closed drawer, fruit from closed fridge, both closed at end, 4500 steps). 5 clutter objects per receptacle.
- Robot: Fetch, wheeled base + 7-DoF arm, parallel-jaw gripper; head + arm RGB-D 90 deg FoV 128x128; EE max 1.5 cm/step; grasp possible within 15 cm. Navmesh agent radius and Fetch reach not stated (unverified).
- Success: task = targets within 15 cm of goal (orientation ignored); episode fails on accumulated contact force over threshold (Pick 5k N, Place 7.5k N). Navigation skill: within 0.3 m and 0.5 rad of goal + stop, 500 steps. Pick: 200 steps, return to rest pose holding target. Open drawer: 90-100% open; close fridge: within 0.15 rad of closed.
- Articulated spawn regions (opening clearance proxy): open fridge robot spawns in a 0.9 m x 1.2 m region in front of the fridge (leaving it fails); open drawer 0.15 m x 0.75 m rectangle. Pick spawn noise 0.1 m base / 0.3 rad / 0.05 m EE.
- Failure causes: SPA fails to find a goal configuration ~3x more on PrepareGroceries than TidyHouse (cluttered fridge); planning timeout 30 s; per-cause percentages only in a figure (not transcribed). Learned termination costs ~20% nav success.
- Relevance: gives the classic "base pose region in front of an articulated object" (0.9 x 1.2 m fridge) and the 15 cm placement tolerance; clutter (5 per receptacle) is a deliberate difficulty knob.
