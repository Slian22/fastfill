# BEHAVIOR Robot Suite: Streamlining Real-World Whole-Body Manipulation for Everyday Household Activities
- Authors: Yunfan Jiang, Ruohan Zhang, Josiah Wong, Chen Wang, Yanjie Ze, Hang Yin, Cem Gokmen, Shuran Song, Jiajun Wu, Li Fei-Fei. arXiv Mar 2025 (v2 24 Aug 2025). https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05652
- What: whole-body teleop (JoyLo) + WB-VIMA policy on Galaxea R1; motivated by an analysis of BEHAVIOR-1K activities -> 3 capabilities: bimanual coordination, stable/accurate navigation, extensive end-effector reachability.
- Height prior (Fig. 2): "Multiple distinct modes appear in the vertical distance distribution, located at 0.09 m, 0.49 m, 0.94 m, and 1.43 m ... heights at which objects are typically found" (reference point not stated; floor implied, unverified). No horizontal statistics, no per-capability task fractions.
- Robot (Galaxea R1, App. A): omnidirectional base, max 1.5 m/s forward/lateral, 3 rad/s yaw, ground clearance 30 mm; 4-DoF torso, fully extended 340 mm wide x 1223 mm tall (excl. head); two 6-DoF arms, 923 mm full reach each, 5 kg payload/arm; "effective reach range from ground level to 2 m vertically" and up to 2.06 m horizontally; gripper stroke 0-100 mm. Head ZED 2 depth 0.2-3 m, wrist ZED-Mini 0.1-1 m. Base footprint not stated.
- Tasks (real): clean the toilet, lay clothes out, put items onto shelves, take trash outside, clean house after a wild party; 15 evals each; sub-task (ST) and end-to-end (ET) success, no numeric pass thresholds.
- Results: "average success rate of 88% in short-horizon sub-tasks", "peak success rate of 93% in long-horizon full tasks", avg ET 58%; 13x DP3, 21x RGB-DP on ET.
- Failure causes: DP3 collided with tables; ACT hit doorframes; RGB-DP over-force; limitation: camera FOV mismatch, compounding errors.
- Relevance: gives the reach envelope (0-2 m vertical, 0.923 m arm) for an R1-class robot and a measured household-object height distribution; doorframe and table collisions are the reported layout-related failures.
