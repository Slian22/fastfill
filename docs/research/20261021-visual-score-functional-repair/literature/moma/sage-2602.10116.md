# SAGE: Scalable Agentic 3D Scene Generation for Embodied AI
- Authors: Hongchi Xia, Xuan Li, Zhaoshuo Li, Qianli Ma, Jiashu Xu, Ming-Yu Liu, Yin Cui, Tsung-Yi Lin, Wei-Chiu Ma, Shenlong Wang, Shuran Song, Fangyin Wei (NVIDIA, UIUC, Cornell, Stanford). Feb 2026 (v1 10 Feb, v2 20 Feb). https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10116 (HTML v2 read)
- What: agentic generation of indoor rooms/multi-room layouts with open-vocabulary styles; SAGE-10k = 10k scenes, 50 room types, 50 styles, 565K unique 3D objects; mean 48.2 objects per scene (column not defined in text).
- Validity: collision ratio of meshes (trimesh); unstable if "relative translation exceeds 0.2 meter or its rotation exceeds 8 degrees after 120 simulation steps". No navigability/reachability/clearance metric, robot radius or grid size found.
- Robot: "a Franka Emika Panda arm mounted on an Omron LD-60 mobile base". Tasks: Pick-and-Place (mug -> bowl) and Mobile Manipulation (navigate, pick coke can, move to another desk, place). Demos: M2T2 grasps, cuRobo trajectories, RRT navigation on a "2D occupancy grid" (size not given); failed demos filtered by collision checks and placement verification (rate not reported). >28k pick-place demos (264 objects); ~50k MoMa demos across 50 scenes; Diffusion Policy (MoMa = four sequential policies).
- Success (Table 4, train/test): privileged agent PnP 65.3 / 57.7, MoMa 68.4 / 52.8; policy PnP 63.4 / 50.0, MoMa 54.6 / 46.0.
- Failure causes: "Motion planning is not always successful, often due to inaccurate grasp pose predictions, unreachable target configurations, or unexpected collisions during trajectory execution"; policy failures "mostly due to the randomness of the policy inference, far-away objects, or difficult grasp pose location" (App. A.2.2). No quantitative breakdown.
- Relevance: generated scenes used directly for MoMa data; even a privileged planner fails ~1/3-1/2 of MoMa episodes, with unreachable configurations and far-away objects named, so per-target base-pose feasibility is a missing scene-side check.
