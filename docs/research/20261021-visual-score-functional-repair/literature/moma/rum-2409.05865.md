# Robot Utility Models: General Policies for Zero-Shot Deployment in New Environments
- Authors: Etukuru, Naka, Hu, Lee, Mehu, Edsinger, Paxton, Chintala, Pinto, Shafiullah (NYU, Hello Robot, Meta). arXiv Sep 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05865
- What: five single-skill policies trained on diverse handheld-tool demos, run on Hello Robot Stretch (iPhone wrist camera) with a GPT-4o success verifier + retry.
- Eval: 25 unseen environments (5 per task) x 10 trials; 2,950 real rollouts in total across New York, Jersey City, Pittsburgh.
- Success with retry: door opening 47/50, drawer opening 47/50, tissue pickup 46/50, reorientation 43/50, bag pickup 42/50 = 225/250 = 90%; without retry 74.4%; 1.31 tries per success; verifier false positives ~4.8%.
- No navigation: robot starts in the task space facing the target, from a grid of start positions (no numeric ranges given).
- Scene causes: doors/drawers need long handles (round knobs excluded); two close handles confuse grasping; cloth drawers pulled out (no force feedback); objects roll/fall off surfaces.
- Relevance: cabinet/drawer opening is ~94% once the robot is placed facing the handle -> the layout's job is to guarantee a frontal approach pose and swing space, not the skill itself.
