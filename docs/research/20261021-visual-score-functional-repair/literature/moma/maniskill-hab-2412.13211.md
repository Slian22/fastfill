# ManiSkill-HAB: A Benchmark for Low-Level Manipulation in Home Rearrangement Tasks
- Authors: Arth Shukla, Stone Tao, Hao Su (Hillbot Inc., UC San Diego). arXiv v1 9 Dec 2024, v3 28 Feb 2025 (venue not stated on arXiv page; ICLR 2025 unverified). https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13211
- What: GPU-accelerated re-implementation of HAB (TidyHouse, PrepareGroceries, SetTable) in ManiSkill3 with low-level whole-body control, RL/IL baselines, and rule-based event-labelled trajectory filtering for demo generation; 10,000 train / 1,000 val episode configs per task.
- Robot: Fetch; arm, torso, head pan/tilt actuated (PD delta joint position), base driven by linear/angular velocity; head + arm 128x128 RGB-D; sim 100 Hz, control 20 Hz. Navigation is replaced by teleport (not learned).
- Success: Pick = grasped, EE within 0.05 m of rest, arm deviation <= 0.6, static, cumulative collision <= 5000 N; Place = object within 0.15 m of goal, released, EE <= 0.05 m of rest, collision <= 7500 N; Open = 75% of range (fridge) / 90% (drawer), collision <= 10,000 N; Close = within 1% of closed; subtask episodes 200 steps.
- Base placement at episode start: "Spawn robot facing x, within 2 m of x, with noise, and without collisions" (pick/place); Open/Close spawn region "[0.933,-0.6]x[1.833,0.6]" in front of fridges, "[0.3,-0.6]x[1.5,0.6]" in front of drawers (i.e. ~0.93-1.83 m / 0.3-1.5 m ahead, +-0.6 m lateral; interpretation of the rendered notation is mine). Base noise +-0.2 m, rotation +-0.5.
- Failure causes: Pick failures split into excessive collision / mobility (cannot reach object) / can't grasp / drop / too slow. Pick Cracker Box (train): TidyHouse RL-All success 29.46%, excessive collision 34.52%, can't grasp 28.17%; PrepareGroceries RL-All 11.51% success, 60.62% excessive collision (cluttered fridge).
- Layout failure (load-bearing): "the Close Fridge policy completely fails on validation scenes because the fridge door opens into a wall" -> Close Fridge val 0.00% (RL-Per), 0.00% (IL), 0.10% (DP) vs train 86.81 / 86.90 / 64.09%. Clutter in fridge shelves forces tighter place tolerance; first fridge pick disturbs neighbouring objects.
- Relevance: the clearest documented case of a layout (door swing into wall) making an articulated task 0% solvable; supports an explicit door/drawer swing-clearance check and a base-pose region in front of openables.
