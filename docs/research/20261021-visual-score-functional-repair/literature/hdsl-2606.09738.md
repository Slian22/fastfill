# HDSL: A Hierarchical Domain-Specific Language for Structured 3D Indoor Scene Generation and Localized Editing with LLM Agents
- Authors: Letian Li, Chao Shen, Shuzhao Xie, Chenghao Gu, ZhengXiao He, Yu Meng, Xin Yang, Wenyuan Jiang, Zhi Wang. 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09738

## CLIP
- Prompt text, CLIP variant and views: NOT specified (unverified). CLIP similarity between rendered images and text prompts.
- Table 1 avg: Holodeck 19.81, I-Design 19.58, HDSL 20.99 (per room range 9.23-26.05; Office: I-Design 15.93 vs HDSL 10.31). Ablations 16.96-20.76; other LLMs 20.97-23.54. Differences of ~1 point with huge per-room variance.
- Authors: CLIP "is a proxy for text-scene alignment", to be paired with human preference and geometry metrics (OOB, PIoU, NObj). No CLIP-human correlation reported; 20-participant Bradley-Terry study (120 votes) not quantitatively linked.

## Relevance
Shows CLIP scores in this literature are small-margin, high-variance and unvalidated against humans.
