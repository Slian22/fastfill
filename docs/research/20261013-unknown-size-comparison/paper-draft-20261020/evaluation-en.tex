\section*{Experiments and Evaluation}

\paragraph{Task and protocol.}
Given room geometry, fixed objects and a furniture-category list, FastFill estimates furniture dimensions from a training-only prior and uses a 27B placement model to predict positions, orientations and support relations. Dimensions remain fixed during deterministic repair (S1) and optional local-LLM refinement (O2). We report the model, repair and refinement stages separately on the same \NumRooms{} test requests. These are descriptive results on a previously exposed test split, not a new blind benchmark. The original matched 8B joint-size/placement study and its registered endpoints remain a separate experiment.

\paragraph{Metric selection.}
Prior work combines distributional image quality with task-specific checks: ATISS and DiffuScene report rendering-based distributional metrics; LayoutGPT also reports room-boundary violations; OptiScene combines FID, object overlap and visual/human judgments~\cite{atiss2021,diffuscene2024,layoutgpt2023,optiscene2025}. Accordingly, we emphasize \emph{geometric success with a size-error check}, and report downstream admission and visual quality separately. Table~\ref{tab:metrics-en} fixes the terminology and denominators; no metric is retrospectively promoted to a preregistered endpoint.

For a complete room $r$ with $n_r$ objects, the two continuous geometry metrics are
\begin{equation}
 E_r=\frac{1}{3n_r}\sum_{i=1}^{n_r}\sum_{d\in\{x,y,z\}}
 \left|\log\frac{\hat{s}_{rid}}{s_{rid}}\right|,
 \qquad
 \oor_r=\frac{\sum_{i<j}|A_{ri}\cap A_{rj}|}{\sum_i|A_{ri}|}.
\end{equation}
Here $A_{ri}$ is the axis-aligned footprint formed from the predicted center and width/depth \emph{without applying yaw}. All movable pairs enter OOR, without support or height exemptions. OOR is neither an out-of-room rate nor a collision probability, and can exceed one. GSR instead uses oriented geometry with fixed tolerances and semantic collision exemptions. Strict admission uses its own fixed adaptation, including boundary snapping even for the row labeled ``raw''; it is neither a RoomGenBench standard nor the human usability rate (UR) in OptiScene. Human UR has not been measured.

\paragraph{Separate visual evaluation.}
Table~\ref{tab:visual-en} concerns the \emph{old checker-trigger system}, not the new trigger. Reference and generated scenes share assets, cameras and rendering settings. FID/KID use 2,048-dimensional Inception features and one fixed perspective per room. Of 1,009 selected rooms, 1,005 are successfully rendered/analyzed; four lack a required asset. Mesh collision-free means no eligible non-parent movable-object pair exceeds $500\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ voxel overlap (2\,cm voxel pitch), rather than exact zero intersection.
