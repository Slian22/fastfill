# Literature context for the V1.1 stage comparison

Verified on 2026-10-11 by opening each primary arXiv abstract page; the DPO reference implementation and official LayoutGPT/InstructScene project/repository pages were also discovered or opened. This is a compact bootstrap survey, not an exhaustive novelty review. Paper claims below are paraphrased from primary abstracts; their experiments were not reproduced here.

| Work | Verified metadata and primary source | Relevance and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model | Rafael Rafailov, Archit Sharma, Eric Mitchell, Stefano Ermon, Christopher D. Manning and Chelsea Finn. First submitted 2023-05-29; latest arXiv revision 2024-07-29. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290), [reference implementation](https://github.com/eric-mitchell/direct-preference-optimization). | Reformulates preference-based alignment with a classification objective, avoiding a separately fitted reward model and reinforcement-learning loop. It motivates the sequence-level objective and reference-policy comparison. It does not show that automatically assigned geometric preferences are correct or that DPO necessarily improves this layout task. |
| LayoutGPT: Compositional Visual Planning and Generation with Large Language Models | Weixi Feng, Wanrong Zhu, Tsu-jui Fu, Varun Jampani, Arjun Akula, Xuehai He, Sugato Basu, Xin Eric Wang and William Yang Wang. First submitted 2023-05-24; NeurIPS 2023. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15393), [official project](https://layoutgpt.github.io/). | Uses in-context visual demonstrations represented as style-sheet-like text to elicit layout planning, including 3D indoor scenes and numerical/spatial relations. It shows why structured numeric generation and instruction fidelity are relevant, but its data and inference setting are not a ready-made matched baseline for the current corpus. |
| InstructScene: Instruction-Driven 3D Indoor Scene Synthesis with Semantic Graph Prior | Chenguo Lin and Yadong Mu. Submitted 2024-02-07; ICLR 2024 spotlight. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04717), [official repository](https://github.com/chenguolin/InstructScene). | Combines a semantic graph prior with a layout decoder for controllable scene synthesis and constructs instruction-scene pairs. It motivates explicit relation evaluation in addition to collision counts. Its graph/diffusion architecture is distinct from the current autoregressive JSON model. |

The local research gap is empirical: we do not yet know whether this already trained V1.1 SFT model benefits from these two audited preference stages on full, fixed cohorts without reducing coverage or scale quality. That is a testable engineering question; it is not an established literature novelty gap.

A collision-oriented label can reward shrinking furniture or ignoring a prompt relation. That concern arises from the local audited pairs and evaluator behavior; it is not being attributed as a result of the papers above. Consequently this experiment audits preference semantics and reports size, constraints and raw model outcomes alongside repaired geometric acceptance. A future publication claim would require a broader current literature search, matched external baselines and repeated seeds.
